The Price

Same Curriculum, Very Different Price Tags

California requires three courses — Real Estate Principles, Real Estate Practice, and one elective — at 45 hours each, from a DRE-approved provider. That's the whole requirement, and it's identical everywhere: a $100 package and a $400 package teach to the same state-mandated curriculum. What the extra money buys is production quality, support, and study tools — worth paying for if they help you actually finish, but never different material.

Most online students land in the low end of the range. Classroom-based real estate classes, where you can still find them, tend to cost more and move on the school's schedule instead of yours.

The Real Timeline

The Clock Nobody Advertises

Here's what the marketing pages leave out. California sets a legal minimum of 18 days per course, and the courses must be taken one at a time — you can't enroll in all three and knock them out in parallel. That's the famous ~54-day education floor. But the floor is only the beginning:

Each course ends in a proctored final exam, and with online schools that means scheduling a webcam-proctored session — often a day or two out, whenever the proctoring calendar has an opening. After you finish, a human reviews your session before your result becomes official, which can take another day or two. Only when your course completion certificate arrives can you start the next course. Repeat that cycle three times, and the "54-day" education realistically runs eight to ten weeks for most people — before the exam application process even begins.

💡

Use the forced pace as free prep time. The waiting days between courses are built-in study days — drill practice questions on the topics you just covered while they're fresh, and you'll walk out of the 135 hours already exam-ready instead of starting your real studying from zero.

Online vs. Classroom

It's (Almost) All Online Now

Most Californians now do the entire education online — the courses, the course finals (webcam-proctored from home, often in the school's own locked-down exam browser), and the certificates, all without leaving the house. The only step that must happen in person is the state exam itself, at a DRE exam center. If you were searching for a fully online California real estate license: the education half is, the exam half isn't.
